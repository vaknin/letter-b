The letter B b — teacher's plan

3rd grade · EFL beginners · 45 minutes · Site: vaknin.github.io/letter-b

Goals: pupils hear and say /b/ at the start of a word, recognise and write B and b, know 6 words (book, backpack, ball, bus, banana, balloon) and ask and answer "What is it?" – "It's a ___."

Bring: a real backpack with a book, a ball, a banana and a balloon inside (for the lead-in and the feely bag), one soft ball for Hot potato, the textbook page for writing.

Before class (3 minutes)

  • Open the site on the classroom computer in Chrome or Edge and connect the projector.
  • Press F for full screen.
  • Click the speaker at the top (or Test sound in the menu): you should hear a ding and an English voice. Turn up the speakers now, not in the middle of the lesson.
  • Press M for the menu of all stages. The bar under the top shows where you are; green parts are offline stages.

Keys that work everywhere

  • PgDn / PgUp (or the arrows at the top right): next / previous stage. Most clickers send these.
  • 1–19: jump to a stage (19 = bonus). M: menu. F: full screen.
  • Space: reveal / play. ← →: next item inside a game. R: say it again.
  • Everything also works by tapping, so a pupil can come to a touch board.

Closing pair activity: Bus stop (stage 17)

Pairs. Pupil A secretly picks a word and shows it with a gesture or a quick drawing. Pupil B asks "What is it?", then guesses "It's a banana!". Then they swap. Press Space to start the bus timer; when the bus reaches the BUS STOP sign (3 minutes) it honks and everyone freezes.

Walk around and listen for the full sentence. Pick one pair to show the class at the end.

One tip

Keep the computer's voice as the model, and your own voice for praise. After every word the computer says, pause one beat and have the class say it back before you press the next key. That beat is where they actually practise the /b/.
