The letter B b — teacher's plan

3rd grade · EFL beginners · 37 minutes · Site: vaknin.github.io/letter-b

Goals: pupils hear and say /b/ at the start of a word, recognise and write B and b, know 6 words (book, bag, ball, bus, banana, balloon) and ask and answer "What is it?" – "It's a ___."

Bring: a real bag with a book, a ball, a banana and a balloon inside (for the lead-in), and one soft ball for Hot potato.

Three phases of learning a word

  • Repetition pupils hear the word and say it after the model, many times.
  • Recognition pupils pick out the word they hear or see, without saying it yet.
  • Reproduction pupils say, spell or use the word on their own.
Each stage below shows its phase.

Before class (3 minutes)

  • Open the site on the classroom computer in Chrome or Edge and connect the projector.
  • Press F for full screen.
  • Click the speaker at the top (or Test sound in the menu): you should hear a ding and a child's voice. The voice is recorded into the page, so it works on any computer, offline too. Turn up the speakers now, not in the middle of the lesson.
  • Press M for the menu of all stages. The bar under the top shows where you are; green parts are offline stages.

Keys that work everywhere

  • → next: the next item inside a stage, and after the last item the next stage. ← goes back the same way. Most clickers send these. The arrows at the top right jump a whole stage.
  • 1–16: jump to a stage (16 is the bonus). M: menu. F: full screen.
  • Space: reveal / play. Enter: answer. R: say it again.
  • Everything also works by tapping, so a pupil can come to a touch board.

Closing pair activity: Bus stop (stage 14)

Pairs. Pupil A secretly picks a word and shows it with a gesture or a quick drawing. Pupil B asks "What is it?", then guesses "It's a banana!". Then they swap. Press Space to start the bus timer: the bus drives along the road to the BUS STOP sign, and when it gets there (3 minutes) it honks and everyone freezes.

Walk around and listen for the full sentence. Pick one pair to show the class at the end.

One tip

Keep the computer's voice as the model, and your own voice for praise. After every word the computer says, pause one beat and have the class say it back before you press the next key. That beat is where they actually practise the /b/.
